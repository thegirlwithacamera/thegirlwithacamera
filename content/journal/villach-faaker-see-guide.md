*I worked with Region Villach Tourismus and Naturel Hoteldorf Schönleitn during this stop. The opinions and the photographs are my own.*

Villach was a stop on my Interrail route, between Munich and Slovenia. It is a very cute little town. And when you reach the lake and the hotel village, it feels like a whole other world.

## Villach overview

**How we got there:** by train from Munich, with our Interrail pass. Munich to Villach was the most beautiful ride of the whole trip.

**How long we stayed:** three nights.

**How we got around:** by bus, between the hotel, the lake and the town.

**How long I would stay:** it is clearly the kind of place where you can spend one or two weeks, just to switch your brain off.

## Where to stay: Naturel Hoteldorf Schönleitn

A hotel village of chalets in Latschach, at the foot of the Mittagskogel, above Lake Faak.

**What I loved:** everything. The breakfast, with good products. The staff. The chalet.

## Best things to do in Villach and Lake Faak

### Walk around the old town of Villach

We walked all around the town.

**The umbrella street:** as soon as you enter the old town, it is on your right. It is Lederergasse, one of the oldest streets in Villach, covered with more than 200 colourful umbrellas as a summer installation. Look for the flood marks on the houses, like the one from 1567 at number 12.

![The umbrellas of Lederergasse in Villach](/images/portfolio/travel/villach/10.jpg)

![A doorway in the old town of Villach](/images/portfolio/travel/villach/3.jpg)

### Swim in Lake Faak

We swam at the private beach of the hotel group, and it was amazing. The water is turquoise.

**Good to know:** guests of Naturel Hoteldorf Schönleitn can use the private beach of its sister village, Naturel Hoteldorf Seeleitn, in Egg am Faaker See, about 2 km away.
