Venice was the last stop of our Italy trip in October 2024, after Naples and Florence. We were there on 11 and 12 October.

I fell in love with Venice.

## Venice overview

**When:** 11 and 12 October 2024.

**How long we stayed:** two days.

**How we got around:** on foot.

**How long I would stay:** three to four days.

## Where to stay in Venice

We stayed at the Residenza San Maurizio, in the San Marco sestiere, a few minutes from the Accademia bridge and about ten from Saint Mark's Square.

## Best things to do in Venice

### Walk the alleys, early

The alleys are my photo spot in Venice, and the whole point is to be out before everyone else.

**My advice:** get up early. Venice at 7am and Venice at 11am are not the same city.

![In a Venice alley](/images/journal/venice/green-curtain.jpg)

## Where to eat and drink in Venice

### Ristoteca Oniga

On Campo San Barnaba, in Dorsoduro, across the Grand Canal from Saint Mark's. A seafood place on a small square, and one of the best meals of our trip.

**Good to know:** the sign calls it a ristoteca, an osteria and an enoteca all at once. The menu is Venetian and changes with the season, the tables spill out onto the campo, and the dining room is small, so book. Dorsoduro in general is where you find the good tables, away from the tourist route.

## Things to know before you go

- **Get up early.** It is the single best piece of advice I can give for Venice.
- **Walk.** The centre is small, and the vaporetto is mostly useful for the islands.
- **Check the tides in autumn.** October to December is the acqua alta season, when high water can flood the lowest squares for a few hours.

## How many days in Venice?

Three to four days, with one of them for the islands.

## Venice FAQ

**How many days in Venice?**
Three to four days, including a day for Burano and Murano.

**How much is the vaporetto in Venice?**
€9.50 for a single ticket valid 75 minutes, €25 for 24 hours. For an island day, take the pass.

**When is the best time of day in Venice?**
Early morning. The alleys are empty and the light is soft.

**Where to stay in Venice?**
We stayed at the Residenza San Maurizio, in San Marco.
