Bratislava was a stop on my Interrail route, between Vienna and Prague. We arrived at 11am and left at 3pm, so four hours in the city, with our bags on our backs.

Bratislava is super safe, super clean and super cheap.

## What we did in four hours

### The old town

Small, walkable, and easy to do with luggage. That is what we did: we walked it with our bags.

### The Blue Church

The Church of Saint Elizabeth, the art nouveau church entirely covered in pale blue, from the walls to the roof tiles.

**Good to know:** it was built between 1909 and 1913 by the Budapest architect Ödön Lechner, who also designed the grammar school next door in the same style. Entry is free, but the church is only open around services, roughly 6:30 to 7:30am and 5:30 to 7pm, and Sunday mornings. In summer there are guided visits of the interior through the day. Outside those hours you photograph it from the street, which is what most people come for anyway.

### The basketball court next to the church

My photo spot in Bratislava: the basketball court right beside the Blue Church.

![The basketball court next to the Blue Church](/images/journal/bratislava/basketball-court.jpg)

### 17's Bar, for a spritz and wings

We stopped at 17's Bar for a spritz and some chicken wings, which cost almost nothing compared to Vienna.

**Good to know:** 17's Bar is on Hviezdoslavovo námestie, the long square between the old town and the Danube, with tables outside.

## Should you stop in Bratislava?

You should stop, and you should sleep there. Four hours was not enough.

**Good to know:** Vienna to Bratislava takes about an hour by train, with departures through the day, so it also works as a day trip. If you do stop between two trains, the main station has luggage lockers, and the old town is about twenty minutes away on foot.

## Bratislava FAQ

**Is Bratislava worth visiting?**
Yes. Super safe, super clean and super cheap. Stop, and stay the night.

**How long do you need in Bratislava?**
More than the four hours we had. At least one night.

**What should you see in Bratislava in a few hours?**
The old town and the Blue Church.

**How far is Bratislava from Vienna?**
About an hour by train.
