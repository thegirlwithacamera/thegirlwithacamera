We stayed two nights at Hotel Adler, in Menaggio, in September 2026, while we did the central triangle of Lake Como.

> A COMPLETER : en une ou deux phrases, ce que tu as ressenti en arrivant.

## The hotel

Via Luigi Cadorna 91, Menaggio.

**What it is:** a family run hotel on the hill above Menaggio, with a large garden, a swimming pool looking out over the lake and the mountains, and a restaurant. Breakfast is a buffet. Rooms are classic rather than designed, some with a balcony, with a view of either the lake or the mountains.

**What made it:** the owner. He is adorable, he speaks French, and it is a genuinely good hotel.

**Where it puts you:** on the hill, not on the water. Count five minutes by car or about thirty five on foot down to the centre of Menaggio and the ferry landing, with the bus stop right outside the door. The Menaggio and Cadenabbia golf club is 800 metres away.

**Good to know:** free parking in front, which matters on this lake, and staff who know the local walking trails. Check in from 3:30pm, check out by 10am.
