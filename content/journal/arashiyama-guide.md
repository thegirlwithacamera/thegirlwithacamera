We went to Arashiyama on the morning of 4 November 2025, from Kyoto.

The bamboo forest is very impressive.

## Arashiyama overview

**When:** the morning of 4 November 2025.

**How long we stayed:** a morning, and half a day is plenty.

**How we got there:** by Uber, there and back.

**Good to know:** the usual way is the JR Sagano line from Kyoto station to Saga-Arashiyama, about 15 minutes for 240 yen, or the Randen tram from the Shijo area. At 5am, before the first trains, a taxi or a ride app is the only option.

## What we did

We did everything except the little train.

### The bamboo grove, before sunrise

We got up very early, for sunrise, to avoid the crowds. That is the whole point of Arashiyama.

**Good to know:** the grove is free, open day and night, and there is no ticket. Before 8am the path is quiet, and you can actually hear the bamboo. By 10am it is packed and photographing it becomes difficult.

### The monkey park

**Good to know:** the Arashiyama Monkey Park Iwatayama is on the other side of the river, south of Togetsukyo bridge. Entry is 800 yen, cash only at the gate, and the climb takes 20 to 30 minutes uphill for 160 metres of elevation. Around 120 wild macaques live at the top, with a view over the whole of Kyoto. You feed them from inside a hut, not out in the open.

**My advice:** do not be scared of the monkeys, they are really chill.

### The temples and the bridge

Tenryu-ji and its garden, and the Togetsukyo bridge over the river.

**Good to know:** Tenryu-ji opens at 8:30am, 500 yen for the garden, 300 more for the buildings. The bridge is free.

## Where we ate

Back in Kyoto, after the morning.

## What I would tell you

- **Come very, very early.** It is the difference between having the bamboo to yourself and queuing for a photo.
- **Watch the stairs.** I went down hard on them.
- **Do not be afraid of the monkeys.** They are calm, and the rules are there to keep it that way.

## How long do you need in Arashiyama?

Half a day is plenty.

## Arashiyama FAQ

**When should you visit the bamboo grove?**
Before sunrise, or at least before 8am. After 10am it is crowded.

**Is the bamboo grove free?**
Yes, and it is open at any hour.

**How much is the Arashiyama monkey park?**
800 yen, cash only at the gate, plus a 20 to 30 minute climb.

**Are the monkeys dangerous?**
No. They are wild, but calm. Follow the rules at the feeding hut and it is fine.

**How do you get to Arashiyama from Kyoto?**
JR Sagano line to Saga-Arashiyama, about 15 minutes. Before the first trains, take a taxi.
