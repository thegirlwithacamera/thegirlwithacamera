We went to Faro for Valentine's Day 2025, for three days and two nights. It is very chill, and a real change of scenery.

## Faro overview

**When:** February 2025, around Valentine's Day.

**How long we stayed:** three days, two nights.

**How long I would stay:** four to five days.

## Where to stay in Faro

We stayed at **Alto House Faro**, on Rua de Berlim, a renovated guest house about fifteen minutes on foot from the old town, with free bikes and breakfast.

For Valentine's Day, the hotel had prepared the room for us: red roses, a bottle of Portuguese sparkling wine, two glasses, a box of chocolates and a bowl of strawberries.

[Check prices and availability at Alto House Faro](https://www.kqzyfj.com/click-101884973-15734897?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.booking.com%2Fhotel%2Fpt%2Falto-house-faro.html)

**My tip:** sleep in Faro itself. It is much cheaper, and you can do day trips to the neighbouring villages from there.

## Best things to do in Faro

### The old town

The Cidade Velha, the walled old town, is small and easy to walk: you enter through the Arco da Vila gate, and you end up on Largo da Sé, the cathedral square lined with orange trees, with the old bishop's palace on one side.

### The beach

Praia de Faro, the long sandy beach on the barrier island in front of the city.

![Fishermen on Praia de Faro](/images/journal/faro/praia-de-faro.jpg)

![The dunes behind the beach](/images/journal/faro/beach-dunes.jpg)

### The Ria Formosa nature reserve

The lagoon and its islands, between the city and the sea. It is a natural park, with birds, salt pans and boats out to the islands.

![The wooden boardwalk across the Ria Formosa](/images/journal/faro/ria-formosa-boardwalk.jpg)

### The red cliffs of Praia da Falésia

The cliffs in the town next door, near Albufeira, with their red and ochre walls dropping into the sand.

![On the red cliffs of the Algarve](/images/journal/covers/my-cameras-cliffs.jpg)

## Where to eat in Faro

### Restaurante Chefe Branco

Rua de Loulé 9, near the marina. We went back the next day, because it was that good.

**Good to know:** Chefe Branco is a local institution for fish and seafood, known for its cataplana, the Algarve fish stew served in a copper pot, and for its grilled fish and meat. Portions are generous and prices stay reasonable.

### Vila Adentro, for a cocktail on the roof

Praça Dom Afonso III 17. We had a cocktail on the rooftop terrace of Vila Adentro, in the Cidade Velha, right next to the church and a few steps from Largo da Sé, the cathedral square lined with orange trees.

## Things to know before you go

- **Sleep in Faro, not in the resort towns.** It is much cheaper.
- **Plan day trips** to the neighbouring villages, and keep Faro as your base.
