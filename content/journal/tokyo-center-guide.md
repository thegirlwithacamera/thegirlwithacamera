The center was several days of my twelve days alone in Tokyo. Each morning I picked a direction, took the metro out there, and walked.

**Where I walked:** Akihabara and Kanda; Jimbocho and Ochanomizu; Tokyo Station, Yaesu and Nihonbashi; Otemachi and Marunouchi; Kagurazaka.

## Akihabara and Kanda

Akihabara is known for electronics, anime and maid cafés. In the morning, before the shops open, it is another city: delivery vans, shops open 24 hours, and office workers on their break.

**Tokyo Camera,** Taito 1-13-8, seven minutes on foot from Akihabara station. Used film cameras, medium format and vintage lenses, with a warranty. Tax free.

## Jimbocho, the booksellers' district

Jimbocho has around 170 second hand bookshops, about a third of all the second hand bookshops in Japan. The district grew up around the universities at the end of the 19th century. Bookshops put their shelves and boxes out on the pavement, and people browse in the street, even in the rain. Jimbocho station (Toei and Tokyo Metro) or Ochanomizu station (JR).

The bookshop I had photographed there on my first trip, the photo that went viral on Instagram, no longer exists.

## Ochanomizu, the guitar street

A few minutes from Jimbocho, Meidai-dori climbs towards Ochanomizu station: one guitar shop after another, new and used, on several floors. Just behind, the Kanda river runs in a deep green trench, with the yellow Sobu line trains passing above.

## Kagurazaka

Kagurazaka is called Tokyo's little Paris: French schools, French restaurants and bakeries, next to old ryotei and small stone paved alleys from the time of the geisha. Kagurazaka station (Tozai line), Iidabashi or Ushigome-Kagurazaka.

## Tokyo center FAQ

**Where to buy a used film camera in Tokyo?**
In Akihabara, Tokyo Camera has used film cameras, medium format and vintage lenses.

**How many bookshops are there in Jimbocho?**
Around 170 second hand bookshops.

**Why is Kagurazaka called little Paris?**
Because of its French schools and restaurants, next to the old geisha alleys.
