## Tsukiji outer market

It was great. An incredible atmosphere.

**Good to know:** the fish auctions moved to Toyosu in 2018, but the outer market stayed, with its fishmongers, knife shops and food counters. Most shops open from about 5am to 2pm. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays, and many shops also close on some Wednesdays. The closest stations are Tsukiji Shijo (Oedo line) and Tsukiji (Hibiya line). Go early.

## Daimon and Hamamatsucho

**Câlin,** a natural wine bar. Hamamatsucho 1-18-10, one minute from Daimon station, exit A2. Every day, 2pm to 11pm. A really lovely place. I was the only customer, so I chatted with the barman. You can also buy bottles to take away.

**Film:** I had my films developed at two Palette Plaza shops, one here in Shiba-Daimon and one in Ebisu. About an hour of waiting each time, and they even printed a few photos for me as a bonus, because they liked what I was shooting.

## Gotanda and Musashi-Koyama

On the morning of 21 June I was at the Oi Racecourse flea market (it gets its own article). Then I walked north, through Osaki and Gotanda, to Musashi-Koyama. I wanted to discover, so I walked, and I came across these shopping streets.

**Musashi-Koyama Palm** is the longest covered shopping arcade in Tokyo: about 800 metres and more than 200 shops, right next to Musashi-Koyama station on the Tokyu Meguro line. It opened in 1956, one of the first big covered arcades in Japan.

## Ebisu

Lunch at **Le Bar à Vin 52 Azabu Tokyo**, an Italian wine bar on the 4th floor of Atre Ebisu West, one minute from the west exit of Ebisu station. Every day, 11am to 11pm, lunch until 3pm. A 300 yen table charge after 4pm. Then Palette Plaza to pick up my photos, and a head spa at **LIBERTA Tokyo,** Ebisu 1-11-3, 3rd floor.

## Tokyo south FAQ

**Is the Tsukiji market still open?**
Yes. The wholesale market moved to Toyosu, but the outer market is still there. Go early, and avoid Sundays and public holidays.

**What is the longest shopping street in Tokyo?**
Musashi-Koyama Palm, a covered arcade of about 800 metres next to Musashi-Koyama station.

**Where to escape the tourist areas in Tokyo?**
Nakameguro and Daikanyama: much more real and simple.
