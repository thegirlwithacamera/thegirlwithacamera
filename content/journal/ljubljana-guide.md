I had no particular expectations for Slovenia. Vienna and Prague are world famous, Ljubljana was just a stop on the way. It ended up being the surprise of the whole Interrail trip.

What struck me first is how walkable the city is. Ljubljana feels designed for people before cars. I felt good there, and I felt safe. We ate well, we drank well, and it is a beautiful city that stays simple and affordable.

## Ljubljana overview

**How we got there:** by train, with our Interrail pass, after a stop at Lake Bled.

**How long we stayed:** about a day and a half. It was not enough.

**How long I would stay:** two full days.

## Where to stay in Ljubljana

### Dragon Dream capsule hotel

It was funny to find a Japanese concept in the middle of Slovenia.

**What I liked:** it is really cheap, very comfortable, and the bed was big enough for two people.

**Location:** a little outside the centre, but with a bus stop right in front. The historic centre is 8 minutes away.

**What could be better:** the air conditioning did not work well, and the bathrooms were only cleaned quickly in the morning.

## Things to know before you go

- **Walk.** The historic centre is very pedestrian friendly, you will do everything on foot.
- **Stay outside the centre and take the bus.** It is much cheaper, and the connection is quick.
- **Cards are accepted almost everywhere,** except at the market. Bring some cash for it.

## Best things to do in Ljubljana

### The Central Market

My favourite playground for street photography.

The market is organised by category: food in the centre, flowers in a small alley, and clothes on the side.

### The flea market

I also went to a flea market in Ljubljana, on a Sunday morning by the river. It is very much about antiques: tableware, jewellery, old objects.

![A stall at the Sunday flea market in Ljubljana](/images/journal/slovenia/ljubljana-antiques-market.jpg)

### Walk along the river

We walked along the quays of the Ljubljanica.

### Ljubljana Castle

We took the funicular up to the castle, and walked back down through the woods.

### The old town and the bridges

This is where I filmed the most: the small streets of the historic centre, and the bridges.

## Where to eat and drink in Ljubljana

### Kodila Gourmet & Bistro Market

Right by the Central Market, under Plečnik's arcades. We had čevapčiči, the small grilled sausages served in bread.

### REX Tiskarna

Dunajska cesta 123, next to our capsule hotel. Pizza and pasta. There was a one plus one free offer on pizzas, and I had truffle pasta and a burratina.

![Truffle pasta with burratina and pizzas at REX Tiskarna](/images/journal/slovenia/rex-tiskarna-truffle-pasta.jpg)

### Spritz everywhere

Hugo, Aperol, and the rest. We had spritzes a bit everywhere.

## Ljubljana FAQ

**Is Ljubljana worth visiting?**
Yes. It is walkable, safe, affordable, and it was the surprise of my trip.

**How many days in Ljubljana?**
Two full days.

**Is Ljubljana expensive?**
It felt affordable to me, especially if you stay outside the centre.

**Do I need cash in Ljubljana?**
Mostly for the market. Cards work almost everywhere else.
