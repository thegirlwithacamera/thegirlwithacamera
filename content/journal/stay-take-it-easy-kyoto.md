We rented one of the "Free bike and free WIFI, Take it easy" apartments, at 12-22 Murasakino Nishifujinomori-cho, in Kita Ward, from 1 to 6 November 2025. Ours was number 204.

> A COMPLETER : en une ou deux phrases, ce que tu as ressenti en arrivant.

## The place

**What it is:** a small apartment rather than a hotel room, with its own kitchen, a washing machine, free bikes and a pocket wifi. The owner runs several flats in the same building, each listed separately, so check the numbers when you book.

**The owner:** lovely, and that is half of why the stay worked.

**Where it puts you:** Nishijin, the old weavers' quarter in the north west, quiet and residential. Three minutes on foot from Funaoka Onsen, the bathhouse that accepts tattoos, five from Sarasa Nishijin, the cafe inside a former sento, and about fifteen from the Golden Pavilion.

**How you get around from there:** the bikes. Kyoto is flat, and Nishijin is far enough from the centre that walking everywhere costs you time.
