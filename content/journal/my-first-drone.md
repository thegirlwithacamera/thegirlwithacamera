Until this summer, I had never flown a drone. I wanted aerial shots for my travel films, with as few permit constraints as possible.

## Choosing

I hesitated for a while. In August I found two deals in Belgian shops: a DJI Mini 3 Fly More Combo with the DJI RC remote, and a DJI Lito 1 Fly More Combo.

The Mini 3 was not really an option in the end: the model is no longer made, and what was in the shop window were only display dummies.

So I went with the **DJI Lito 1**, and I bought it on my birthday. It weighs under 250 grams and is a C0 class drone, and it turned out to be really beginner friendly.

## Before the first flight: registering

In Belgium, I registered as a drone operator on the Drones portal of the federal Mobility service (SPF Mobilité), with my insurance details. My status was active on 7 August 2026.

Rules depend on the country, the drone and where you fly, so check the official rules for your own situation before you take off.

## What I brought back

Two weeks later I was on my Interrail journey, and the drone came with me. I brought back a sunset over the city, red rooftops, and the castle of Lake Bled above the water.

You can see those shots moving at the top of my [Destinations](/en/destinations) and Journal pages.

## What I'd tell another beginner

- If, like me, you want as few constraints as possible, look at drones under 250 grams, and check what that means where you live.
- Do the paperwork before the trip, not at the airport.
- Keep your clips longer than you think. A two second shot looks great until you try to loop it.
