I edit everything in Premiere Pro. Reels, travel films, hotel and destination videos: all of it.

## Why I switched to Premiere Pro

I moved to Premiere Pro in 2026, when my editing ambitions grew. Before that, I used Premiere on my phone, for really basic edits.

**Good to know:** Premiere on iPhone is free as a standalone mobile editor. The desktop version is a paid subscription.

## My editing workflow, step by step

### 1. Sort the footage by project, from the backup

My footage is always sorted by project, right from the moment I back up my memory cards.

### 2. Import everything and select the best shots

Next, I import everything into Premiere and go through all the footage to pick the shots I want to keep.

### 3. Tell the story

It is only after this selection that I start telling the story.

### 4. Choose the music last

Only then do I choose a piece of music that matches my vision, and not the other way around. I know that almost everyone in video picks the music before they start editing. I do the opposite.

### 5. Colour grade by hand

Then I colour grade, by hand.

### 6. Add a voiceover and subtitles if needed

Finally, if the video needs it, I add a voiceover, subtitles, and the rest.

![My Premiere Pro timeline, with Lumetri Color open](/images/journal/edits/premiere-pro-cover.jpg)

## What I love about Premiere Pro

**It looks intimidating, but it is very intuitive once you are in it.** Especially the keyboard shortcuts.

My basics, three single keys:

- **C** to cut, with the Razor tool.
- **V** to select, with the Selection tool.
- **B** for the Ripple Edit tool: it brings the rest of the timeline back to the previous shot, so there is no gap left.

## What I like less

- **The price.** It is not cheap.
- **The text tool.** I am not a fan of it, for example to add a title.

## My computer

I edit on a MacBook Air, bought in March 2026. Against all odds, it runs Premiere Pro very well.

## Premiere Pro price

As a single app, Premiere costs US$22.99 a month in the United States, on an annual plan billed monthly. It includes Premiere on desktop and iPhone, plus the Adobe Express Premium plan. Prices vary by country, so check Adobe's site for yours. There is a 7-day free trial, and Premiere is also included in the Creative Cloud Pro plan, with more than 20 Adobe apps.

## Who is Premiere Pro for?

I think you need to love video editing to use software like this.

## Final thoughts

Sort by project, select, tell the story, then music, colour, and subtitles. Premiere Pro looks scary at first, but once you are inside, the shortcuts make it feel natural.
