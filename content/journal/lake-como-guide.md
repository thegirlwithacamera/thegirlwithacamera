Lake Como is incredibly beautiful.

We stayed in the central triangle, the one formed by Varenna, Bellagio and Menaggio, the three villages that face each other where the lake splits in two.

## Lake Como overview

**When:** 22 and 23 September 2026.

**Where we slept:** Hotel Adler, in Menaggio.

**How we got around:** the ferries across the triangle.

**How long I would stay:** a week, easily, if you change village every day.

## The three villages

### Varenna, my favourite

The coloured houses are what did it. Varenna climbs straight out of the water in pink, ochre and green, and the streets are stairs.

![Varenna seen from the water](/images/journal/lake-como/varenna-from-water.jpg)

![The coloured houses of Varenna](/images/journal/lake-como/varenna-houses.jpg)

![A boat in front of Varenna](/images/journal/lake-como/varenna-boat.jpg)

We visited **Villa Cipressi** and its terraced gardens, and the gates that open straight onto the lake, the ones people call the gates of heaven. That is the photo everyone comes for, and it is worth being there before the first boats.

![The gates of Villa Cipressi, open onto the lake](/images/journal/lake-como/cipressi-gates.jpg)

![The pergola in the gardens](/images/journal/lake-como/cipressi-pergola.jpg)

![The pier at Varenna](/images/journal/lake-como/varenna-pier.jpg)

**Good to know:** Villa Cipressi is a late Renaissance villa turned hotel, but the gardens are open to visitors for a few euros, terraced down to the water between cypresses and magnolias. The lakefront path next to it is the Passeggiata degli Innamorati, the lovers' walk, which runs from the ferry landing along the shore.

### Nesso

The village in the gorge, on the western shore south of Menaggio, with its stone bridge over the waterfall and its houses stacked above the water. It is smaller than the other three and far less walked.

**How we got there:** by bus, along the western shore from Menaggio. The buses run all day and stop on the main road above the village, then it is stairs down to the water.

## Getting around: the ferries

The three villages are linked by the ferries of the central lake, and crossing from one to the next takes about fifteen to twenty minutes.

![On the ferry deck](/images/journal/lake-como/ferry-deck.jpg)

![Inside the ferry](/images/journal/lake-como/ferry-inside.jpg)

**Good to know:** Varenna to Bellagio and Bellagio to Menaggio are the two short hops, and the car ferry also takes foot passengers. Buy at the landing stage, check the last departure of the evening before you settle in for a spritz, and remember that timetables thin out a lot outside the summer season.

## Things to know before you go

- **Give yourself time, and patience.** Everything moves at the speed of the boats, and so do the crowds.
- **Get up early.** Very early. It is the only way to have Varenna and Bellagio to yourself.
- **Buy a silk souvenir.** Silk is the speciality of this lake, and it has been for centuries.

## How long do you need at Lake Como?

A week, easily, if you change village every day. Two days gives you the triangle and nothing else.

## Lake Como FAQ

**How do you get to Nesso?**
By bus along the western shore. It is not on the ferry triangle.

**Which village should you stay in?**
We slept in Menaggio and my favourite to walk was Varenna, for its coloured houses.

**How do you get between Varenna, Bellagio and Menaggio?**
By ferry, fifteen to twenty minutes between each.

**What should you buy at Lake Como?**
Silk. It is the local speciality.

**How long do you need at Lake Como?**
Two days for the central triangle, a week if you want a village a day.
