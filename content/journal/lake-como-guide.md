Lake Como is incredibly beautiful.

We stayed in the central triangle, the one formed by Varenna, Bellagio and Menaggio, the three villages that face each other where the lake splits in two.

## Lake Como overview

**When:** 22 and 23 September 2026.

**Where we slept:** Hotel Adler, in Menaggio.

**How we got around:** the ferries across the triangle.

**How long I would stay:** a week, easily, if you change village every day.

## The three villages

### Varenna, my favourite

The coloured houses are what did it. Varenna climbs straight out of the water in pink, ochre and green, and the streets are stairs.

I visited the villa, and went to the Gates of Heaven.

### Menaggio

Same, and it is where we slept.

## Getting around: the ferries

The three villages are linked by the ferries of the central lake, and crossing from one to the next takes about fifteen to twenty minutes.

**Good to know:** Varenna to Bellagio and Bellagio to Menaggio are the two short hops, and the car ferry also takes foot passengers. Buy at the landing stage, check the last departure of the evening before you settle in for a spritz, and remember that timetables thin out a lot outside the summer season.

## Things to know before you go

- **Give yourself time, and patience.** Everything moves at the speed of the boats, and so do the crowds.
- **Get up early.** Very early. It is the only way to have Varenna and Bellagio to yourself.
- **Buy a silk souvenir.** Silk is the speciality of this lake, and it has been for centuries.

## How long do you need at Lake Como?

A week, easily, if you change village every day. Two days gives you the triangle and nothing else.
