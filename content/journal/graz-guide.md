*I worked with Graz Tourismus during this stop. The opinions and the photographs are my own.*

Graz was a stop on my Interrail route, between Ljubljana and Vienna. We only stayed 24 hours, and that was not enough.

What I remember most: a very peaceful and pleasant city.

## Graz overview

**How we got there:** by train from Ljubljana, with our Interrail pass.

**How long we stayed:** 24 hours, with one night in an apartment in Lend.

**How long I would stay:** two to three days.

## Where to stay in Graz

We stayed in an apartment in Lend, the neighbourhood across the river from the old town. It was very pleasant.

## Best things to do in Graz

### The Schlossberg

The hill in the middle of the city, with its clock tower. This is where I saw the red rooftops of Graz at sunset.

We took the funicular up.

![The red rooftops of Graz from the Schlossberg](/images/journal/graz/schlossberg-view.jpg)

![Graz from the Schlossberg at the end of the day](/images/journal/graz/schlossberg-trees.jpg)

![The clock tower on the Schlossberg](/images/journal/graz/clock-tower.jpg)

**Good to know:** the Schlossbergbahn funicular climbs the hill in a few minutes, with a 60 percent gradient and a glass roof. It runs every 15 minutes, from 9am to midnight from Sunday to Thursday, and until 2am on Friday and Saturday. All Graz public transport tickets for zone 1 are valid on it. You can also walk up, or take the Schlossberg lift.

### The Kunsthaus

The contemporary art museum, nicknamed the "Friendly Alien" for its shape, on the river bank in Lend. I saw a beautiful exhibition about flowers there.

**Good to know:** in 2026, the museums of the Universalmuseum Joanneum, which includes the Kunsthaus, have a year-long theme called BLOOM, around blossoming in art, nature and culture. The Joanneum museums are closed on Mondays.

### The old town

We walked through the old town. For photos, my favourite spot in Graz is simply the city in general.

## Good to know: the Graz Card

The Graz Card works for 24, 48 or 72 hours, and costs €32, €42 or €47. It includes public transport in zone 101, the Schlossbergbahn funicular and the Schlossberg lift, free entry to the Kunsthaus and the other Joanneum museums, and a guided walk through the old town. If you already have a transport ticket, the cheaper Graz Card Light has the same museum benefits without public transport.

## Where to eat in Graz

### Café Restaurant Demiri

On Lendplatz, in Lend. A small restaurant known for its ćevapčići, the small grilled sausages.

### Der Steirer

Belgiergasse 1, in Lend. We had dinner there, and I had a goulash.

**Good to know:** Der Steirer is known for Styrian cooking and its "Styrian tapas", small seasonal plates. It is also open on Sundays.

## How many days in Graz?

Two to three days. Stay more than 24 hours: we did not have enough time.

## Graz FAQ

**Is Graz worth visiting?**
Yes. It is a very peaceful and pleasant city.

**How many days in Graz?**
Two to three days.

**How do you get up the Schlossberg?**
By the Schlossbergbahn funicular, the Schlossberg lift, or on foot.

**Is the Graz Card worth it?**
If you plan to take the funicular, public transport and visit the Kunsthaus or other Joanneum museums, it covers all of them.

**Where to eat ćevapčići in Graz?**
At Café Restaurant Demiri, on Lendplatz.

**Where to stay in Graz?**
We stayed in an apartment in Lend, a pleasant neighbourhood close to the Kunsthaus.
