Two weeks, twelve stops, one bag.

## The bag

Everything fitted in my Stubble & Co 20L backpack, and it was incredible.

## The capsule

- Black bermuda shorts
- Black cycling shorts
- A black polka dot Adidas skirt
- A white tee with puff sleeves
- A white lace tank top
- A pink textured top
- A red blouse
- A black bralette
- A black sheer textured top
- The Adidas jacket that matches the polka dot skirt, also worn as a small vest

## The piece I wore the most

The bermuda shorts. Pockets are life, and skirts do not have them.

## The one thing to bring besides clothes

A power bank.
