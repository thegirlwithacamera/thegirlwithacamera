## The market

The Tokyo City Flea Market takes over the car parks of Oi Racecourse, a horse racing track by the bay in Shinagawa. On busy days, there are up to 600 stalls, mostly private sellers: clothes, tableware, prints, old watches, toys, bags, and a lot of things you did not know you were looking for.

## My advice

Go early, and dig. It is clearly worth it.

## Practical information

**When:** most weekends, from 9am to 2:30pm, but many sellers start packing up around noon. The market can be cancelled in case of rain, and dates can change: check the official Instagram, @tokyocity_fleamarket, before you go.

**Entry:** free.

**Getting there:** Tokyo Monorail to Oi Keibajo-mae station, two minutes on foot. About 20 to 30 minutes from central Tokyo.

**Pay:** bring cash, many stalls do not take cards.

**Wear:** comfortable shoes. The market is huge.

## Oi flea market FAQ

**When is the Oi Racecourse flea market?**
On most weekends, from 9am to 2:30pm. Check @tokyocity_fleamarket before you go, as it can be cancelled in the rain.

**How do you get to the Oi Racecourse flea market?**
By the Tokyo Monorail, to Oi Keibajo-mae station.

**Is it worth it for vintage designer pieces?**
Yes, if you go early and dig. I found a Chloé Woody, a Prada bag and a Vivienne Westwood necklace.
