In June 2026, I spent twelve days in Tokyo on my own, from 18 to 29 June.

The first thing I felt was on the train from the airport into the city: it was like coming home. I felt I was in the right place.

In twelve days, I really took the time to discover the many sides of Tokyo. No list of main attractions: each morning I picked a direction, south, east, west or north, took the metro or a train out there, and from there I walked.

## Tokyo overview

**When:** from 18 to 29 June 2026, on my own.

**Where I stayed:** Juyoh Hotel, Kiyokawa 2-15-3, Taito, about ten minutes on foot from Minowa station. Futons, shared bathrooms.

**How I explored:** one direction a day, by metro or train, then on foot.

## Where the walking took me

**Nippori Fabric Town,** the fabric district. Around ninety shops of fabrics, trimmings and sewing supplies, in Arakawa, near Nippori station. Most open from about 9am to 7pm; hours on Sundays vary from shop to shop.

**Ochanomizu,** the guitar district. The guitar shops line Meidai-dori, a few minutes on foot from Ochanomizu station.

**Jimbocho,** the booksellers' district. The bookshop I had photographed there, the photo that went viral on Instagram, no longer exists.

**The hydrangeas along the Toden Arakawa line.** In June, a road of hydrangeas follows the tram tracks.

## Taking care of myself

- **LIBERTA Tokyo,** a head spa. Ebisu 1-11-3, 3rd floor.
- **ASSORT Tokyo,** a hair salon with English-speaking stylists. Kita-Aoyama 2-10-28, basement, four minutes on foot from Gaiemmae station. Closed on Mondays.
- **A second tattoo by Tiemi** at PopInk Tokyo, Arakicho, Shinjuku. My first one is in the [first timer guide](/en/journal/tokyo-first-timer-guide).

## Where I ate and drank

- **House of the White Dragon,** a tiny Japanese curry place in a basement in Kabukicho, Kabukicho 2-45-5, decorated like a fantasy tavern. I finally made it there.
- **Câlin,** a natural wine bar. Hamamatsucho 1-18-10, one minute from Daimon station, exit A2. Every day, 2pm to 11pm. A small counter, and you can also buy bottles to take away.

## Getting film developed in Tokyo

I had my films developed twice, at two different Palette Plaza shops: one in Shiba-Daimon, one in Ebisu. I can only recommend it. About an hour of waiting, and they printed some photos for me as a bonus, because they liked what I was shooting.

There are several Trefac shops in Koenji. Mine is the big one, on a corner.

## Tokyo FAQ

**How do you explore Tokyo without a plan?**
Pick a direction each morning, take the metro or a train out there, and walk.

**Where can you get film developed in Tokyo?**
I went to Palette Plaza, in Shiba-Daimon and in Ebisu: about an hour of waiting.

**When do the hydrangeas bloom along the Toden Arakawa line?**
In June.
