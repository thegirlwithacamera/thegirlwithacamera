We were in Osaka from 6 to 10 November 2025, with a day trip to Nara in the middle, and a 6am train to Tokyo on the last morning. So three full days in the city.

What I liked is the contrast: the castle on one side, Dotonbori on the other, and in between a much quieter city.

## Osaka overview

**When:** 6 to 10 November 2025.

**How long we stayed:** three days, which felt right.

**Where we slept:** an Airbnb.

**How we got around:** on foot.

## Best things to do in Osaka

### Walk outside the tourist quarter

This is what I would tell anyone: just walk, away from the tourist streets. Osaka is far more than Dotonbori.

### Osaka Castle

**Good to know:** the park around the castle is free and open all day. The keep itself costs 600 yen, and it is a reconstruction with a museum inside and a viewing deck at the top. The park is at its best early, before the groups.

### Dotonbori

The neon, the canal, the Glico running man, the giant crab. It is worth seeing, but it is one street, and it is not the city.

**Good to know:** it is at its most photogenic after dark, when all the signs are lit, and at its worst in the early evening, when it is packed.

### Vintage shopping

Osaka has a lot of vintage shops, and it is the reason I am writing a separate guide about them.

**My advice:** if you love vintage, save your wallet for Osaka. Do not spend it all in Tokyo.

## Where we ate: Iccho

A tiny yakitori counter in Higashishinsaibashi, run by a couple in their seventies and eighties. We found it by chance, and it was the best meal of our stay.

**Good to know:** Iccho seats eight people at a counter, opens from 6pm to 10pm, and is closed on Sundays. It is cash only, with a seat charge of 330 yen per person and one drink per person. Book ahead if you can: it fills up fast.

## Things to know before you go

- **Go beyond Dotonbori.** Osaka is much more than that one street.
- **Save your vintage budget for Osaka**, rather than Tokyo.
- **Walk.** The centre is flat and dense, and the good parts are between the sights.

## How many days in Osaka?

Three days was good, with a day trip to Nara on top.

## Osaka FAQ

**How many days do you need in Osaka?**
Three days in the city itself is a good amount, plus a day for Nara.

**Is Dotonbori worth it?**
Yes, once, at night. But it is one street, and Osaka is much bigger than that.

**Where should you go vintage shopping in Japan?**
Osaka. Keep your budget for it rather than spending it in Tokyo.

**How much is Osaka Castle?**
The park is free, the keep is 600 yen.

**Where to eat yakitori in Osaka?**
Iccho, in Higashishinsaibashi, a counter with eight seats run by an older couple. Cash only, closed on Sundays.
