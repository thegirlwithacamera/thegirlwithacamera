At the end of August I left Brussels with an Interrail Global Pass. Two weeks later I came back through the same station with twelve stops behind me: Frankfurt, Munich, Salzburg, Villach, Faaker See, Bled, Ljubljana, Graz, Semmering, Vienna, Bratislava and Prague.

This is the route, what worked, and what I would do again.

[Watch the film of the journey](/en/destinations#interrail)

## The route, day by day

### Brussels to Munich

Day one was the longest: Brussels, a change in Frankfurt, then Munich. Two nights there, walking the city and shooting the first images of the trip. One address that surprised me: La Fayette, a surprisingly good spot. I had planned a day trip to Neuschwanstein and let it go.

![Looking out of the window on the first day](/images/journal/interrail/train-window.jpg)

### Salzburg, Villach and Faaker See

From Munich the train crossed into Austria. I stopped in Salzburg for a few hours, then carried on down to Villach. Munich to Villach was the most beautiful ride of the whole journey. I had planned two nights and stayed longer than planned, between the old town, the lake and the mountains.

![Villach, Carinthia](/images/portfolio/travel/villach/1.jpg)

[See the Villach project](/en/photographer/travel/villach) and [Naturel Hoteldorf Schönleitn, by Faaker See](/en/photographer/hospitality/naturel-dorf-schonleitn)

### Bled and Ljubljana

Lake Bled is incredibly beautiful. It was hot, so we went for a swim.

![Rowing boats on Lake Bled](/images/journal/interrail/bled-boats.jpg)

Ljubljana was the surprise of the trip. I arrived on a Friday evening and stayed two nights at Dragon Dream, a capsule hotel, which turned out to be a really nice, comfortable experience. The central market and the river are where I spent my time. The light on the market is hard, so go early.

![A Sunday morning market in Ljubljana](/images/about/path/now-market.jpg)

Where I ate: Kodila gourmet, Bistro Market, and the Central Market itself.

### Graz

One night in Graz, in an apartment in Lend. What I remember: the red roofs at sunset seen from above, and a beautiful exhibition about flowers at the Kunsthaus.

![Graz from above](/images/portfolio/travel/graz/4.jpg)

[See the Graz project](/en/photographer/travel/graz)

### Over the Semmering to Vienna

Graz to Vienna, with a stop on the Semmering line. The Semmering line is worth watching from the window. Then four nights in Vienna.

![A train crossing a viaduct on the Semmering line](/images/journal/interrail/semmering.jpg)

[Read my Vienna guide](/en/journal/vienna-slow-guide)

![A fiaker in front of St. Stephen's Cathedral, Vienna](/images/journal/interrail/vienna-stephansdom.jpg)

### Bratislava and Prague

On the way from Vienna to Prague, I stopped in Bratislava for a few hours.

![Bratislava](/images/journal/interrail/bratislava.jpg)

Prague was the last stop, three nights. Two addresses I loved there: Mandlárna and V Domově.

![Early morning in Prague](/images/journal/interrail/prague.jpg)

A small warning: keep an eye on your taxi app and read the bill in restaurants, a service charge can be added for you.

### Back to Brussels

The return was one long day on three trains: Prague to Berlin, Berlin to Cologne, Cologne to Brussels. I left Praha Holešovice at 6:41 in the morning and was home at 17:35.
