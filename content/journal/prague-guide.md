*I worked with At the Golden Pear during this stay. The opinions and the photographs are my own.*

Prague was the last stop of my Interrail journey, after Vienna and a few hours in Bratislava. We stayed three nights.

I will be honest: my first feeling, arriving at the train station, was a strong sense of insecurity.

## Prague overview

**How we got there:** by train from Vienna, with a stop in Bratislava, with our Interrail pass.

**How long we stayed:** three nights.

**How long I would stay:** three days, like we did, is enough to see what there is to see.

## Where to stay in Prague

### At the Golden Pear, in the castle district

We stayed in the castle district, a five minute walk from the castle, at At the Golden Pear.

Fun fact: it is also a beer spa, and beer is unlimited for the whole stay.

**Good to know:** the hotel is in Nový Svět, the old Hradčany quarter just a few minutes' walk from Prague Castle, the Loreto and Strahov Monastery. It is a listed 17th century house, from the time of Emperor Rudolf II.

## Where to eat and drink in Prague

### Mandlárna

A great café, in the castle district too. Really delicious. It was the first place in Prague with excellent service, and the prices are more than fair.

**Good to know:** Mandlárna, on Loretánská street, is a café and shop where everything revolves around almonds.

![A café front in the castle district](/images/journal/prague/cafe-front.jpg)

### Restaurace V Domově

U Uranie 21, in Holešovice, Prague 7. A typical Czech restaurant, with only locals. Count around 200 to 300 Czech crowns per person. I had fried Czech cheese, and it was delicious.

But this is also the restaurant that added a 10 percent tip to our bill without telling us.

## What to watch out for in Prague

### Tips

Really watch out for tips. Some places add them to your bill sneakily, or practically demand them. Read your bill before you pay.

### Ride apps

Our Uber driver left without us, and we were still charged for the ride as if we had been in the car. Check your trip in the app before you pay.

### Money: card or cash

The Czech Republic has its own currency, so we paid with a Revolut card. Many places are still cash only. We did not bother: we simply did not go to those places.
