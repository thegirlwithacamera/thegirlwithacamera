## What to watch out for in Prague

### Taxis and ride apps

Our ride left without us, and we were still charged for the trip as if we had been in the car. Keep an eye on your app and check the trip before you pay.

### Restaurant bills

One restaurant added a 10 percent tip to our bill without asking. Read the bill before you pay.
