Lake Bled was a stop on our Interrail route, between Villach and Ljubljana. We only had a few hours there, with our backpacks on. The weather was beautiful, and I would give it a full day next time.

## Lake Bled overview

**How we got there:** by train, with our Interrail pass, then by bus to the lake.

**When we arrived:** around 11am.

**How long we stayed:** a few hours, before continuing to Ljubljana the same evening.

**How long I would stay:** one full day.

## Getting to Lake Bled

We arrived at the station around 11am and took the bus to the lake.

**Be careful:** there is only one bus every two hours. Check the timetable before you arrive, so you do not lose half your day waiting.

**Our bags:** we kept our backpacks with us all day. Yes, even for the viewpoint.

## Best things to do at Lake Bled

### Walk around the lake

We walked the whole loop around the lake. It is completely flat, and without photo stops it takes two hours at most.

There were a lot of people because the weather was beautiful, but only at the start of the loop. Most people do not bother to walk all the way around, so the rest of the lake is much quieter.

![Rowing boats on Lake Bled](/images/journal/slovenia/bled-boats.jpg)

![A heart lock on the fence facing Bled Island](/images/journal/slovenia/bled-heart-lock.jpg)

### Mala Osojnica viewpoint

The famous view over the lake and its little island church. I will be honest: I thought I was going to die on the way up, the climb is intense. And we did it with our backpacks.

### Swim in the lake for free

It was hot, so we went for a swim, opposite Camping Bled. It is completely free.

![Swimmers on the wooden deck at Lake Bled](/images/journal/slovenia/bled-swimming.jpg)

### Fly a drone

I flew my drone over the lake. I did not see any signs, and I had checked the map before flying. Rules can change, so check the official drone map before you take off.

### A glass of wine under the yellow parasols

Near the spot where you first reach the lake, there is a small kiosk serving drinks under yellow parasols. Very photogenic. We had white wine, not very expensive, and loved sitting there for a while before heading back.

## Where to eat at Lake Bled

### Restaurant Zaka

Pizza at Restaurant Zaka, at Camping Bled, right by the lake.

## Lake Bled FAQ

**Can you visit Lake Bled as a day trip?**
Yes, by train and bus. But I would give it a full day.

**How long does it take to walk around Lake Bled?**
Two hours at most without photo stops. The path is completely flat.

**Can you swim in Lake Bled for free?**
Yes. We swam opposite Camping Bled, for free.

**Is the Mala Osojnica hike hard?**
It is short but steep. I found it very intense, so go at your own pace.

**Is Lake Bled crowded?**
At the start of the loop, yes. Walk further around the lake and it gets much quieter.

**How often is the bus to Lake Bled?**
When we went, there was one every two hours. Check the timetable before you go.
